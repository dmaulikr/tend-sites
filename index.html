Questions about Tend Garden

What is Tend Garden?

Tend Garden is a free habit tracker for iPhone with no streaks. You plant up to five habits, and each one is a hand-drawn plant that grows through five stages as you tend it each day. Miss a day and the plant rests. It never dies, and there's no streak to break.

Is Tend a habit tracker without streaks?

Yes. There are no streaks, leaderboards or challenges, and notifications stay off unless you turn them on.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your plant rests and waits for you. It never dies.

Is Tend free?

Yes. Planting, tending, widgets and the growth replay are free. The Greenhouse is an optional one-time purchase that adds an AR garden, prints, look-back videos and pot glazes. There's no subscription.

Is my data private?

Everything stays on your iPhone. There's no account, no tracking and no ads.

How many habits can I track?

Up to five, each with its own plant from 12 hand-drawn kinds.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Home Screen widgets let you water a plant with one tap.
